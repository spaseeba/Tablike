Tablike HS230 Stylus

    • Widely Compatibility - USI stylus pen supports Amazon Fire Max 11 tablets and Google Pixel tablet, as well as certain Chromebook models (Lenovo Chromebook Duet/Lenovo Chromebook Duet 3/Lenovo Chromebook Duet 5/Lenovo Chromebook Duet 5/Acer Chromebook Spin 713/Acer Spin 713/Acer Spin 714/HP Chromebook x2 11/Galaxy Chromebook 2) and other Chromebooks that support the USI protocol. Note: Please make sure your device is compatible with the USI protocol, if your device model is not listed in the image, please contact us to confirm compatibility.

     

    • USB C Fast Charging + LED Light - The usi chromebook pen is designed for USB-C fast charging, so you can get up to 120 hours of continuous use from a 20-minute charge. It also has a smart power saving feature that automatically shuts off after 10 minutes of inactivity. There's also an LED charging indicator that turns red to indicate that it needs to be charged or is charging, and green to indicate that it's fully charged and ready to use.

     

    • Palm Anti-Touch Technology - USI 2.0 stylus realizes anti-touch technology through precise sensors and complex algorithms, allowing you to naturally place your palm on the screen as a support point when using the usi chromebook stylus, without worrying about mis-touch interrupting text or drawing input and interrupting your creativity. It simulates a real drawing experience, improve your writing and drawing comfort and efficiency.

     

    • Magnetic Design and Dual Touch Design - USI 2.0 stylus adopts magnetic connection design. You can easily adsorb the stylus on the tablet without worrying about losing or forgetting to bring it. In addition, it also has a dual touch mode, first, the 45mm pen tip, which is wear-resistant and simulates real pen strokes. Secondly, the capacitive pen cap allows sliding and clicking, similar to the operations performed by hand on the smartphone screen.

     

    • USB C Fast Charging + LED Light - The Chromebook stylus adopts USB-C fast charging design, which can be used continuously for 120 hours after charging for 20 minutes. It also has a smart power saving function, which automatically shuts down after 10 minutes of inactivity, greatly extending the battery life. In addition, there is an LED charging indicator light, red means it needs to be charged or is charging, and green means it is fully charged and can be used.

 

What's in the box? 1x USI2.0 Pencil, 1x Replacement Nib, 1x Type-C Cable, and a User Manual. Please contact us if you have questions about product compatibility.
