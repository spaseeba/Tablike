Tablike T906 Drawing Tablet

TABLIKE T906 10x6 Inch Graphics Tablet for Drawing with 8192 Levels Pen Pressure, Digital Tablet for Artist Paiting / Gaming OSU

  • Working Area Configuration - TABLIKE art tablet equips with a 10 x 6 inches working area, providing the user with the most comfortable size to work; the 10mm slim structure and minimalist design of appearance make the drawing tablet more attractive.

  • Tilt Function Battery-free Stylus: This computer graphics tablet come with a battery-free stylus, no need to charge, allowing for constant uninterrupted drawing. ±35° tilt support enables imitation of lines input with diverse drawing gestures, with accuracy ensured.

  • Press Keys:10 programmable press keys, you can set shortcut keys on drawing tablet's driver based on your preferences, such as erase, zoom in/out, scroll up and down, and so on.

  • Compatibility: TABLIKE graphics tablet supports Windows XP, 7,8,10 or later/ macOS 11 or later/ Android 5.0 or later. TABLIKE T906 supports various mainstream design and drawing software, including PS, SAI, AI, CDR, etc.

  • NOTE: You can easily connect your phone to the art tablet via the OTG connector; while iPhone and iPad are NOT at the moment. The cursor will not show up in the SAMSUNG Galaxy S series at present. If you are not sure whether the product is compatible with your Phone or any help, please contact us.

  • Symmetrical Design: The pen tablet can provide a better user experience for both right and left-handed users. If you are left-handed, you can adjust the tablet to 180° in the driver. Ideal for professional artists, beginners and amateur hobbyists of all kinds.

  • Ultra-Thin & Ultra-Light Design: Engineered with aerospace-grade aluminum alloy, this ultra-thin 5mm drawing tablet (99% thinner than standard drawing tablets) redefines portability. At 327g (lighter than a 12oz coffee cup), it effortlessly slips into backpacks or laptop sleeves, ideal for artists on-the-go, students, and remote creators.
